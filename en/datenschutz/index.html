2. Visiting the website (server log files)

When you visit this website, our hosting provider processes technically necessary data (IP address, date and time, page accessed, browser type and operating system) in order to deliver the website and ensure its security. The legal basis is our legitimate interest under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. The data is deleted after a short time unless it is needed to investigate abuse.

3. Contact form and withdrawal form

When you write to us via a form, we process your details (name, email address, message or withdrawal details) in order to handle your request. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR. The details are sent to us by email and deleted as soon as they are no longer required for processing.

Spam protection

To protect our forms from automated programs (“bots”), we use server-side checks only (a check field invisible to humans, a minimum completion time and a limit on requests per hour). For this purpose the IP address is briefly stored on our server in anonymised (hashed) form and deleted after one hour at the latest. No data is transmitted to third parties such as Google. The legal basis is our legitimate interest under Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

4. Cookie policy

This website uses no analytics or tracking services. We only set cookies when you sign in via Discord: a technically necessary session cookie is then stored to recognise you as signed in (plus, for a few minutes, a cookie that secures the sign-in process; in the Reichstag's internal area, on the Imperial map and for suggestions, after an action, a notice cookie for at most one minute that displays the confirmation message). Without signing in, no cookies are set. Your choice of colour scheme (light/dark) is stored in your own browser (local storage) and is not transmitted to us.

Sign-in via Discord

Signing in via Discord is required for the internal area of the Reichstag, for the internal view of the imperial administration, for editing the Imperial map and the organisation charts, and for suggestions. Only when you click “Sign in with Discord” are you redirected to Discord; its privacy policy then applies. From Discord we receive your user ID and display name, and we check against our Discord server whether you are a member and which roles you hold there (what you may do depends on this). We never receive your password.

We store this information (user ID, display name, roles, membership and time of sign-in) for as long as you have an account with us. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (participation in the role-play). On request we will delete your account data. To do so, write to das.imperium.germanicum@gmail.com.

Internal sessions of the Reichstag

The Reichstag's internal area uses the same Discord sign-in. Access is granted only to those who hold a role of the Reichstag (President, deputy, sovereign prince) or administrator rights on our Discord server. We store the scheduled sessions and submitted motions with title, text, time, your user ID and your display name. Motions and agendas are visible only to members of the Reichstag, not publicly. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(1)(b) GDPR (participation in the role-play).

Internal view of the imperial administration

The internal view of the imperial administration uses the same Discord sign-in. Access is granted only to those who belong to an imperial authority on our Discord server, are Crown Chancellor or have administrator rights; each authority sees only its own office. For the customs statistics we record when someone joins or leaves our Discord server, submits an application for entry or is naturalised, each with user ID and name, and for naturalisations also the user ID and name of the naturalising officer. This information is not public; it is visible only in the internal view to the customs administration and the Crown Chancellor. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (participation in the role-play) or (f) (traceability of the administration).

Imperial map

Anyone can view the Imperial map; no data about you is stored when doing so. Only persons with administrator rights on our Discord server may edit it, using the sign-in via Discord. Every saved version of the map is recorded with the time, note, user ID and display name of the editor and can be viewed publicly in the version list so that changes remain traceable. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (participation in the role-play) or Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (traceability). The administrative boundaries come from Eurostat (GISCO, © EuroGeographics) and are stored on our server; viewing the map does not establish any connection to third parties.

Suggestions

Only signed-in members of our Discord server can submit suggestions, using the sign-in via Discord. We store the category, description, time, your user ID and your display name, and later the decision with any reason given. Your suggestion is presented to the person responsible by direct message via our Discord bot; you also receive the decision by direct message on Discord. Suggestions are not public; you can see your own on the “Suggestions” page. The legal basis is Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (participation in the role-play and handling of your request).

5. Fonts

The fonts used (EB Garamond, Source Sans 3) are loaded locally from our server. No connection to third-party servers takes place.

6. External links

Our website links to our presences on Instagram and YouTube. Only when you click such a link is data transmitted to the respective provider; its privacy policy then applies.

7. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and objection (Art. 21 GDPR). You can also lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.
