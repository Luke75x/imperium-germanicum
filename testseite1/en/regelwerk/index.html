Server and role-play

The Rules

The guidelines of the Discord server and the rules of the role-play. Everyone who takes part in the Imperium Germanicum accepts them and puts them into practice.

Contents

  1. IServer guidelines
  2. IIWhen does the role apply?

The authoritative version is the one in the information channel of our Discord server (in German).

Section I

Server guidelines

§ 1

The server is owned by Bastian Hüsch and Karl Benjamin Wollnik, with a 50 % share each.

§ 2

New members of the role-play have a general probationary period of 2 weeks.

§ 3

Insults, hate speech, extremism and sexism are not tolerated.

§ 4

Unauthorised advertising for other projects is not permitted.

§ 5

Spam or any other kind of intrusiveness is not tolerated.

§ 6

We insist on courteous and respectful conduct towards one another.

§ 7

Spectators and guest roles are exempt from fixed-term probationary periods; the probation rules always apply to them.

§ 8

Further rules may be added at any time.

§ 9

Players who break these rules must expect a warning or exclusion from the community.

Section II

When does the role apply?

Role-play management

Every participant in the role-play takes on a role. This role may bear the person’s real name or an invented one; it may also match the real personality or differ from it.

What matters is that on this server we never fully set the role aside. The role always remains, even if somewhat relaxed, so that the nature of the role-play is never rejected.

To tell the areas apart, there are areas in which the role is somewhat relaxed and those in which it applies in full.

Areas with a relaxed role

#klatsch-und-tratsch · #kurpfalz
#Café · #Braustube · #Spielothek

Here the roles are somewhat relaxed but never fully set aside, unless a planned event calls for the strict role. Conversations entirely outside the role are to be held in private.

What “relaxed” means

When greeting and taking leave, the correct form of address is used in the classic manner. In the course of the conversation you may switch to the informal “Du” and talk openly about private matters of real life. The rank structure of the role-play and the dignity that the respective positions carry must still be respected.

The roles are never fully set aside.
