The Imperial Administration

The Imperial Authorities

The Imperial Authorities are all bodies that operate throughout the entire territory of the Empire. Their work extends across the whole Imperium Germanicum.

They serve the Empire and its subjects alike: they protect borders and trade, uphold security and order, and bring aid wherever hardship and danger threaten.

Together with the Chancellery and the Imperial Representation they form the Imperial Administration, headed by the Chancellor as Private Secretary to the Sovereign.

The Chancellor

At the beginning of their reign, the Sovereign appoints a Private Secretary and Chancellor for the Imperial Authorities.

As Private Secretary, the Chancellor is the link between the Imperial Authorities and the Sovereign and is responsible for administering the imperial crown estates and His Majesty's treasury.

The Imperial Authorities

The Imperial Authorities are divided into the authorities for security and public order, the civil Imperial Offices and the foreign service.

The authorities for security and public order wear uniform and have their own ranks; their Directors-General belong to the command staff. The Imperial Offices are headed by Secretaries of State who answer directly to the Chancellor.

Security and public order

Imperial Security Office

KRS
HeadDirector-General of the KRS, currently Field Marshal Ritter von Bielicki

Duties

The Empire-wide, superordinate intelligence service, responsible for maintaining internal and external security.

Gathers and evaluates intelligence and warns the Crown, the Senate and the authorities of dangers before they arise.

Imperial Police Authority

Police
HeadDirector-General of the Police
Career pathFrom Polizeiwachtmeister to Leitender Polizeidirektor

Duties

Upholds public safety and order throughout the Empire, prosecutes crimes and protects the life, limb and property of its subjects.

Enforces the judgments of the Imperial Court of Justice and assists the other Imperial Authorities.

Imperial Customs Administration

Customs
HeadDirector-General of Customs
Career pathFrom Zollwachtmeister to Leitender Regierungsdirektor

Duties

Watches over the movement of goods at the borders of the Empire, levies customs and duties and supervises imports and exports.

Secures the external borders of the Empire and supports the Imperial Office for Immigration in controlling entry.

Imperial Civil Protection

KKS
HeadDirector-General of the KKS
Career pathFrom Schutzmann to Leitender Schutzdirektor

Duties

Prepares the Empire for major emergencies, directs relief when disaster strikes and protects the population from natural forces and calamities.

It commands the Imperial Fire Service, which provides fire protection and technical assistance and stands ready everywhere to rescue in fires, accidents and emergencies.

The Imperial Offices

Imperial Office for Immigration and Citizenship

Immigration
HeadSecretary of State of the Imperial Office for Immigration

Duties

Grants immigrants their visa, examines their applications and carries out their naturalisation as citizens.

Keeps the register of citizens and records expatriation and departure.

Imperial Office for Digital Infrastructure

Infrastructure
HeadSecretary of State for Digital Infrastructure

Duties

Builds and maintains the digital institutions of the Empire: the website, the Empire’s assembly rooms on Discord and the automata of the Crown.

Watches over the data protection of all subjects, so that the Empire manages without tracking and without third-party services.

Foreign service

Imperial Consulate General

Consulates
HeadConsul General, to whom the consuls report

Duties

Represents the Empire and its subjects abroad and maintains relations with friendly states and communities.

The Sovereign appoints and dismisses the consuls; the Chancellor administers the consulates in his name.

The Imperial Representation

The Imperial Representation represents the Sovereign in the member states that have no regent of their own. Its office-holders govern these territories like regents without being regents themselves: they act solely in the name and on behalf of the Crown.

The viceregal representatives are officials who are entrusted by the Sovereign directly with the administration of a territory.

Within their territory they represent the Sovereign directly and carry out his duties in his name.

Once a member state has a regent again, the office of the imperial representative there ends and sovereignty passes to the ruler.

OfficeResponsibilityForm of address
ViceroyRepresents the Sovereign in a kingdom or electorate whose throne is vacant. He is the highest-ranking imperial representative.Your Excellency
Governor-GeneralGoverns a large member state without a regent or several combined territories; governors may be subordinate to him.Your Excellency
GovernorGoverns a single territory without a regent, such as a duchy, a county or a province of a governorate-general.Your Excellency
MagistrateConducts, in the name of the Sovereign, the government of an imperial city that is subject to no ruler.–
MayorHeads a town or municipality in a territory under imperial representation and reports to the responsible magistrate or governor.–

Viceroys, Governors-General and Governors rank immediately after the Chief Imperial Justice in the order of precedence of the Empire and enjoy the dignity of the Crown in the Sovereign’s absence.

Imperial Authorities and constituent states

Within the constituent states, the authority of the Imperial Authorities diminishes, for these are administered by their sovereigns: by kings, princes and the other heads of the constituent states.

There, the Imperial Authorities act chiefly where matters concerning the Empire as a whole are at stake, and respect the sovereignty of the respective ruler over their territory.
