Judiciary of the Empire

The Imperial Court of Justice

The Imperial Court of Justice is the supreme organ of the judiciary of the Imperium Germanicum and decides questions of interpretation of the Imperial Charter and matters of ordinary jurisdiction.

The Imperial Court of Justice consists of a total of 5 Imperial Judges, one of whom presides over the institution as Chief Imperial Judge.

The decisions of the court can overturn those of all other courts within the Imperium Germanicum and are generally not open to challenge by any other institution; only the court itself can reverse its decisions.

Seat of the institution

The Imperial Court of Justice is seated in the Court of Appeal building in the imperial capital, Greater Berlin.
