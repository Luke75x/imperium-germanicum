The Constitution and Institutions

Structure of the State

The Imperium Germanicum has its own constitution, the so-called “Imperial Charter”, an etiquette guide and many state institutions.

On this page
The order of the ReichAll institutions

01Structure of the State

The order of the Reich

Sovereign, Senate, Reichstag and Imperial Court of Justice form the head of the Reich; offices and authorities act on its behalf, and the constituent states mostly determine their internal affairs themselves.

Head of state

The three powers

Offices and authorities of the Reich

Structure

Head of state

The Sovereign

The Sovereign is the official head of state of the Imperium Germanicum; officeholders, officers and judges swear allegiance to him.

Incumbent
His Majesty Alexander IX.
Election
By the Senate from among the 9 prince-electors
Term of office
1 year, re-election possible without limit

Within the Reich

  • Appoints and dismisses the officials, judges and consuls of the Reich.
  • Signs the laws approved by the Senate.
Learn more

Executive

The Imperial Senate

The Imperial Senate is the executive arm of the Imperium Germanicum; it consists of the 9 Prince-Electors of the Empire, who together with His Majesty the Emperor lead the Empire.

Members
The 9 prince-electors of the Reich
Seat
Herrenhaus building, Greater Berlin

Within the Reich

  • Elects the Emperor from among the prince-electors.
  • Votes once more on the laws decided by the Reichstag.
Learn more

Legislative

The Imperial Diet

The Imperial Diet of the Imperium Germanicum is the legislative assembly of all heads of the constituent states or their envoys.

Chair
The President; the office rotates every 3 months
Seat
Reichstag building, Greater Berlin

Within the Reich

  • Votes on potential laws and regulations and forwards its resolutions to the Senate.
Learn more

Judiciary

The Imperial Court of Justice

The Imperial Court of Justice is the supreme organ of the judiciary of the Imperium Germanicum and decides questions of interpretation of the Imperial Charter and matters of ordinary jurisdiction.

Members
5 imperial judges, chaired by the Supreme Imperial Judge
Seat
Kammergericht building, Greater Berlin

Within the Reich

  • Its decisions can overturn those of all other courts within the Imperium Germanicum.
Learn more

Imperial Ombudsman

The Hand of the Emperor

Since 15 October 2026 the King of Hanover has temporarily been the Hand of the Emperor and thus the second-highest office-holder of the Reich.

Incumbent
His Majesty Ernst August VI.
Seat of work
Berlin Palace

Within the Reich

  • Presides over the seat of the Senate administration of His Majesty's government.
Learn more

High command

The Joint Chiefs of Staff

The Joint Chiefs of Staff are an administrative leadership body entrusted directly by His Majesty with the entire command and organisation of all military forces within the Empire.

Commander-in-chief
His Majesty Emperor Alexander IX.
Seat
Bendlerblock, Greater Berlin

Within the Reich

  • His Majesty, together with the Senate, chooses the most capable staff officers of general rank.
Learn more

Intelligence service

Imperial Security Office

The Imperial Security Office, KRS for short, is the empire-wide, overarching intelligence service of the Imperium Germanicum.

Director-General
General Field Marshal Ritter von Bielicki
Seat
KRS headquarters, Greater Berlin

Within the Reich

  • The Senate has granted the KRS far-reaching powers.
Learn more

Administration

The Imperial Authorities

The Imperial Authorities are all bodies that operate throughout the entire territory of the Empire. Their work extends across the whole Imperium Germanicum.

Head
The Chancellor
Structure
Chancellery, imperial authorities, imperial representation

Within the Reich

  • The Chancellor is the link between the Sovereign and the administration.
Learn more

Constituent states

The constituent states

The Empire consists of many constituent states of varying size, which mostly determine their internal affairs themselves.

Electorates
9; their head of state holds a seat in the Senate
Other constituent states
Seat in the Reichstag with their delegation

Within the Reich

  • Imperial law overrides state law where laws differ.
Learn more
