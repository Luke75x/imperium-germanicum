Head of state of the Imperium Germanicum

Alexander IX.

His Imperial and Royal Majesty Alexander IX., by the Grace of God Emperor of the Imperium Germanicum, King of the Westerwald Union, King of Apulia, Archduke of Austria and Prince of Sayn-Wittgenstein-Sayn, is the Sovereign and thus head of state of the Imperium Germanicum.

01The office

The Sovereign

The Sovereign is the official head of state of the Imperium Germanicum; officeholders, officers and judges swear allegiance to him.

The Sovereign appoints and dismisses the officials, judges and consuls of the Empire and summons them at will. He can also pardon convicts and, by decree, make certain decisions temporarily without the Senate.

The Sovereign is the highest representative of the Empire and as such enjoys the highest dignity; the Sovereign always has the right to be heard.

02His Majesty

Biography and character

Alexander IX. was born on 10 May 2004 in Hachenburg, the capital of the Westerwald Union. He has been King of the Westerwald Union since 16 January 2024 and was elected Emperor and Commander-in-Chief of the armed forces by the Senate for 1 year on 14 May 2026.

He is himself one of the 9 Prince-Electors of the Empire and belongs to the Westerwald Union faction.

His Majesty is characterised above all by a calm and level-headed character, but he also has eccentric quirks; for example, cold drinks must always be served with ice, preferably scoops of ice.

His Majesty also places great importance on strict adherence to protocol and etiquette.

His Majesty speaks German and, somewhat haltingly, English, and is currently practising Italian.

03Ex Officio

Emperor, King and Archduke

Ex officio the Sovereign is not only Emperor of the Imperium Germanicum but, in personal union, also King of Apulia.

The Kingdom of Apulia is de jure an independent state but de facto bound to the Empire through administration and the military.

In addition, the Sovereign is ex officio Archduke of Austria; in status, however, Austria is not, like the Kingdom of Apulia, a separate state under imperial control but belongs fully to the Empire.

The combination of the imperial throne, the royal throne and the dignity of Archduke also gives rise to the title (Imperial and Royal Majesty).

04Seat of work and residence

Greater Berlin

His Majesty's seat of work is located, together with the Senate administration, in the Berlin Palace. The private residence of the Sovereign, however, is Charlottenburg Palace, which is also located in the imperial capital.
